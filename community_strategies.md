# Community-Driven Swing Trading Strategies
## Sourced from r/swingtrading, r/Trading

## 🏆 TOP STRATEGIES DISCOVERED:

### Strategy 1: EMA Ribbon Pullback
**Source:** r/swingtrading - "Weekly timeframe, 34/200 EMAs"
**Setup:**
- Wait for pullback to 34 EMA
- 200 EMA as overall trend filter
- Above 200 = Long, Below 200 = Short
**Why it works:** Catches the trend while reducing risk on pullbacks

### Strategy 2: The "Only 2 Indicators" Method  
**Source:** r/swingtrading - "The ONLY 2 Indicators You Need"
**Setup:**
- 8-15 EMA band (fast) above 21-34 EMA band (slow)
- Minimum 4% price movement for magnitude
- Trade pullbacks to fast EMA band
**Why it works:** Simplicity + momentum confirmation

### Strategy 3: Trend Following + Pullback
**Source:** r/swingtrading - "What's your swing trading strategy"
**Setup:**
- Look for higher highs/lower lows on daily
- Drop to 4h for precise entries
- Enter on pullbacks to key MAs or S/R zones
**Why it works:** Aligns with institutional flow

### Strategy 4: Breakout + Pullback Combo
**Source:** r/Trading - "Buying Breakouts and pullbacks"
**Setup:**
- Buy breakouts on volume
- Or buy pullbacks to support
**Why it works:** Two high-probability entry types

### Strategy 5: Position Sizing Rule
**Source:** r/swingtrading community wisdom
**Rule:**
- Max 5-8 positions at once
- Hold for +30% gains
- Ride the trend longer
